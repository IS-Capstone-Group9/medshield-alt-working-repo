In  order  to  achieve  the  general  objective,  the  project  will  seek  to  attain  the  following

 2.  Analyze  product-level  demand  and  profitability  to  identify  high-performing

 4.  Identify  seasonal  variations  and  demand  fluctuations  to  support  proactive

 6.  Implement  a  Decision  Support  dashboard  that  visualizes  key  performance
