# Product Management Frameworks

## Description
Provides the product-management methods used to shape MedShield priorities, roadmaps, and outcome tracking. Use these frameworks when the team must decide what to do next and why it matters. The purpose is to connect work to outcomes, not to turn everything into a planning exercise. In the capstone, product framing helps explain why the dashboard features, reports, and data flows matter to the intended users.

## Workflow
1. Define the outcome the work is trying to improve.
2. Identify candidate features, fixes, or experiments.
3. Prioritize using the project's value, effort, risk, and dependency context.
4. Convert the chosen work into a roadmap item or actionable scope.
5. Track whether the work moved the outcome that mattered.
6. Keep the product story aligned with the business analysis chapter.

## Rules
- Tie work to measurable value, not just feature count.
- Use roadmaps to sequence outcomes and dependencies.
- Use OKRs only when they clarify the goal and measurement.
- Do not prioritize work without understanding the user and business impact.
- Keep discovery notes and decisions visible to the delivery team.
- Be clear about what is a commitment, what is a hypothesis, and what is still under discussion.
- Make sure product framing can be defended in the capstone narrative.

## Project Use
- Use prioritization frameworks when tradeoffs are needed.
- Use OKRs when the team needs an outcome target, not just a task list.
- Keep product framing consistent with the documented MedShield goals.
