## Chapter 1 scope clarification

### Insert after Section 1.3.2 Specific Objectives on PDF pages 11 and 12

The five specific objectives above remain the original commitments of the study.
The implementation and results are evaluated against those commitments rather
than replacing them with narrower objectives after observing the available data.
The current working analysis includes supplied recorded sales from 2017 through
September 2026. This wider reporting window does not retrospectively change the
2021–2025 baseline specified in the original objectives, the December 2026
planning intention, or the actual periods of frozen model evaluations. Each
result identifies its own source population, target, period and evidence status.
Incomplete original requirements are reported as limitations rather than inferred
from the presence of a dashboard screen or a passing technical test.

### Working replacement for Section 1.4 Scope and Limitations on PDF pages 12 and 13

The available-data implementation analyzes MedShield's supplied original sales
records from 2017 through September 2026. It supports recorded itemized net sales,
supported gross profit and matching margin, source-label product contributions,
scoped delivery-area comparisons, retrospective forecasting research and
entered-assumption planning. Financial eligibility and the basis of each measure
remain separate. Gross profit is before operating expenses; it is not company
net profit. A recorded product description or quantity count does not alone
establish a canonical sellable SKU, interchangeable presentation or unconstrained
medicine demand.

CALABARZON, MIMAROPA and Bicol remain the intended geographic scope. Actual
regional outputs include only source labels with recorded delivery-meaning
confirmation. Channel and unresolved labels remain unallocated; their absence
from a regional total is not zero sales. The supplied records do not establish
complete territorial or company reporting. Missing calendar months remain
unavailable. Later source availability does not make older training windows
complete or previously inspected outcomes untouched.

Contract summaries are retained separately from itemized product sales. Their
restricted product breakdown and physical quantities are unavailable and are not
reconstructed. A financial scope that combines summary and itemized amounts
retains its separate reconciliation limitation. The confirmed BUPIRIGHT label
pair has a scoped one-ampoule/box-of-one interpretation in the recorded HOSPITAL
channel. This is not verified geographic delivery evidence or global physical-unit
approval for other products.

Historical DOH and weather observations may support source-labelled context and
separately defined research. A proposed mapping, co-observed month or weather
proxy does not establish an accepted disease/territory join, an issue-time
external forecast input or an official current warning. Research comparisons do
not establish operational model approval. Planning scenarios use explicitly
entered demand, stock, pack, cost and supplier assumptions; they do not establish
verified inventory, lead time, expiry, procurement authority or measured benefit.
The original demand, urgency, inventory-method, warning and recommendation
commitments remain subject to their original evidence and acceptance requirements.

### Working replacement for the execution description in Section 1.5 on PDF pages 14 to 16

The implemented workflow separates controlled data preparation, analytical
storage, offline analysis and dashboard consumption. Original files enter the
Databricks Bronze/Silver/Gold process with source identity, dispositions and
candidate review retained. The Next.js dashboard obtains analytics through the
Express/TypeScript gateway from version-bound Gold contracts. Supabase provides
application identity, roles and audit persistence; it does not supply dashboard
analytical measures. Python preparation/evaluation executes offline rather than
training synchronously in a dashboard request.

Descriptive outputs summarize eligible recorded amounts and commercial
contributions. Diagnostic outputs localize comparable changes and retrospective
patterns without establishing causality. Predictive research reports its actual
target, temporal design, comparators and errors. Prescriptive outputs compare
explicit entered assumptions under the implemented scenario rules. Required
analytical-source failures produce an unavailable state rather than substituted
demonstration values. These capabilities are evaluated separately from the
unfulfilled original operational methods and outcome commitments.

## Chapter 2 method selection clarification

### Working replacement for the synthesis on PDF pages 67 and 68

The reviewed literature motivates a set of candidate forecasting, classification
and inventory methods. Inclusion in the review does not establish that a method
has been implemented, is uniquely suitable, or performs best on MedShield's
available source population. External studies' accuracy values retain their own
targets, units, periods and evaluation designs; they are not MedShield results
or local business acceptance thresholds. The bibliography and the underlying
claims require their own source-level citation audit before final submission.

For the executed research, model choice follows the frozen local comparison and
its stated selection rule. The recorded-label XGBoost experiment and the
financial/unit forecasting studies retain their actual positive and negative
findings. A reference baseline retained because challengers do not qualify is
not an operationally approved model. Disease/weather methods, urgency targets,
EOQ/ROP, linear programming, collaborative filtering and official warning chains
remain distinct original commitments; descriptive rankings or entered-input
scenarios do not demonstrate those methods by substitution.

## Chapter 3 executed methodology

### Working insertion for Sections 3.1 and 3.2 on PDF pages 69 to 85

The implemented study uses the CRISP-DM lifecycle with SEMMA organizing the
analytical preparation and assessment steps. Business requirements and original
objectives define the intended decision; source profiling determines the
population that can actually be analyzed. The original data, provenance and
review dispositions are retained before results are interpreted. Each reported
experiment is linked to its registered inputs, code/runtime identity, source
version, evaluation population and limitations.

The current original Gold itemized candidate contains 40,171 records. The net-sales
population and margin-supported population are defined by separate technical
eligibility conditions and are not interchangeable denominators. All ten local
project CSVs reconcile to uploaded original content through exact-byte or ordered
CSV-field comparison, preserving record order and legitimate repeats. That
reconciliation verifies lineage consistency, not independent physical delivery,
complete reporting or accepted financial release. Corrected content must follow
the controlled versioned intake path; equivalent file content does not require
another Bronze/Silver/Gold run.

Preparation retains legitimate repeated transactions and signed adjustments.
Retry duplicate handling is distinguished from deleting repeated business
observations. Missing or invalid historical values remain unavailable; they are
not silently filled by an untested imputation method. Category review, physical
unit interpretation, buyer channel and confirmed delivery geography retain
their separate evidence. Contract summaries are not allocated to invented
product records. Derived mappings and conditional precision policies do not
change source-recorded facts or eligibility without the applicable review.

### Working replacement for analytical storage claims on PDF pages 73 and 89

Databricks Unity Catalog is the analytical system of record. Version-bound Gold
contracts preserve source identity, quality, candidate status and approval limits
through the gateway and dashboard. Supabase owns authentication, application-role
linkage and audit persistence. A proposed generic PostgreSQL staging or FactOutput
diagram is therefore not a literal description of the deployed analytical store.
Warehouse writes, serving connection and publication are distinct controlled
steps; passing a candidate readback does not authorize financial or model release.

### Working replacement for STL and ABC execution claims on PDF pages 93 to 96

Recorded monthly financial observations are the primary seasonality diagnostic.
The implemented robust monthly STL uses period 12 and seasonal window 13 on
qualifying contiguous observed segments with at least 36 months. Missing months
split fitting rather than becoming zeros. Conditional daily zero/stress analyses
retain their assumptions separately from recorded totals. Full-series retrospective
trend/seasonal components use later observations and are not passed into earlier
forecast origins as though known at issue time.

Observed ABC uses eligible positive contribution, with threshold-crossing labels
retained at the stated 80%/95% bands and signed/zero adjustments reported
separately. Source labels and selected-scope counts are not a fixed portfolio of
3,631 verified SKUs. The independently registered XGBoost ABC experiment is
reported separately from deterministic ABC. It evaluates its held-out recorded-label
task and actual baselines; exact-label disjointness does not establish verified
SKU-family independence, and the negative challenger result does not prove
commercial demand or stock urgency.

### Working replacement for forecasting and external inputs on PDF pages 96 to 101

Every forecast comparison states the target and unit, source/version, training
window, actual origin, target month and calendar lead. Monthly recorded net-sales
PHP research is distinct from fulfilled-ampoule research. Neither is automatically
a complete-company or territorial medicine-demand forecast. Learned transformations
and feature construction must use only origin-available history; full-window
STL and future revisions cannot become historical forecast inputs.

The original DII/RSI Prophet and XGBoost urgency commitments remain explicit.
Any executed external-regressor experiment must establish accepted geography,
provider identity, release timing and feature availability before evaluation.
The full 2021–2025 DII denominator cannot be treated as known at earlier origins
that precede some of those observations. A proposed remedy, such as a frozen
pre-origin or origin-local baseline, requires a separately specified and evaluated
protocol; it is not an already executed result. Observed rainfall in millimetres
is not the original probability of above-normal rainfall and must not be relabelled
as that RSI. A high disease ratio alone does not validate a procurement multiplier.

The current confirmed BUPIRIGHT research covers recorded HOSPITAL-channel
fulfilled ampoules, not a verified territory or unconstrained demand. Its
retrospective one- and three-month comparisons have different small target
cohorts, and fixed-prediction omission checks describe score sensitivity rather
than retraining, cross-validation or future accuracy. The separately registered
prospective design plans 12 issue windows from November 2026 to October 2027.
Inputs stop in the preceding month, so issue-month leads 1/3 are source-relative
leads 2/4. All 96 planned method/lead cases remain unissued. The writer and
Gold adapter pass local synthetic tests; live later-source extraction and actual
prospective scoring have not been performed.

### Working replacement for planning and alerts on PDF pages 101 to 107

The active gateway allocation evaluates entered demand, usable stock, reserve,
pack, supplier-cap and budget assumptions using a bounded greedy procedure.
It compares the stock-only baseline with the entered purchase scenario, preserves
product-specific units and exposes infeasible constraints. It does not certify
a global LP/MILP optimum or implement the original EOQ/ROP/MCDA pipeline merely
because an allocation is displayed.

Verified inventory, supplier lead time, ordering/holding costs, expiry and a
supported physical-demand target are required before operational EOQ, reorder
point, safety-stock or expiry claims. A service-level safety-stock factor does
not by itself demonstrate the original expiry-wastage outcome; expiry and service
outcomes need their own measured evaluation. The ≤5% original wastage target is
retained as an unmet commitment, not derived from a scenario fulfillment score.
Similarly, area commercial rankings are not collaborative filtering, and
historical weather context is not an official current PAGASA Signal-2 warning
chain or validated outbreak alert. No procurement override or automatic order
is claimed for the implemented scenario workflow.

### Working replacement for evaluation and monitoring on PDF pages 113 to 118

Evaluation separates source/financial reconciliation, analytical calculations,
model comparisons, synthetic planning controls, engineering workflow checks and
actual user or academic acceptance. Each result retains its exact population and
the test's actual execution status. A developer/researcher review is not an
attributable client acceptance decision. Unrun tests and unmet original
requirements remain visible rather than being marked complete by documentation.

The original MAPE targets below 15% for the specified baseline and below 20% for
the specified urgency task remain original criteria. MAE, RMSE, WAPE, MAPE and
signed bias are distinct measures. Reporting WAPE does not demonstrate that a
MAPE criterion passed, and an unavailable original task is not a successful
result. Operational MAE/WAPE limits for the newer scoped research remain unset;
they cannot inherit a passing value from the original task or an inspected score.

Future evaluation requires accepted source data and actual issue-time inputs,
with methods and decisions frozen before target access. Missing issues cannot
be backdated; later actuals and restatements retain their own versions. Planned
monitoring or automatic retuning is not described as deployed until its scheduling,
source access, failure behavior and release process are actually implemented
and verified. Current dashboard requests do not retrain a model synchronously.

## Appendix Original specific objectives retained

The following reproduces the original wording from PDF pages 11–12 with whitespace normalization only. It is retained for comparison, not approved as revised wording.

**SO1** To analyze MedShield's 2021–2025 sales performance across CALABARZON, MIMAROPA, and Bicol in order to establish a baseline for all subsequent analyses year-over-year per territory using STL decomposition in the Sales Diagnostic module.

**SO2** To identify MedShield's service territories and client accounts based on sales volume, revenue contribution, and demand growth in order to concentrate capital on revenue-driving SKUs and flag slow movers using 80/20 Analysis in the descriptive layer and ABC classification through the XGBoost classifier in the predictive layer in the Product and Area Prioritization modules.

**SO3** To forecast territory-level pharmaceutical demand for December 2026 and onwards and to provide weighted scoring for product urgency in order to allow procurement teams with quantified demand visibility using Facebook Prophet with DII (Disease Intensity Indicator) and RSI (Rainfall Severity Index) external regressors and XGBoost in the Forecast Modeling module.

**SO4** To optimize procurement decisions in order to reduce losses from expired stock and improve inventory turnover, targeting expiry-driven wastage ≤ 5% of the overall coverage baseline, using EOQ, ROP, and linear programming allocation under MCDA-weighted criteria (revenue rank, demand growth, outbreak risk) in the Prescriptive Planning module.

**SO5** To present five modules, i.e. Sales Diagnostic, Product Prioritization, Area Prioritization, Forecast Modeling, and Prescriptive Planning, in order to enable evidence-based procurement and inventory decisions for MedShield's December 2026 and future planning cycles, using a dashboard with CSV upload validation, PAGASA Signal-2 typhoon alerts, and collaborative filtering recommendations.
