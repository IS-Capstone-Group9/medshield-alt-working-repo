### 4.0.1 Current original-source ABC experiment — 27 October 2026

A registered offline comparison evaluated two fixed XGBoost configurations against
training-majority and prior-year ABC persistence baselines. Targets were annual
positive-contribution ABC classes from the full observed recorded-label portfolio,
with crossing-inclusive 80/95 thresholds. Features used only prior-year financial
history, eligibility counts and observed-month support; training-only imputation
and missingness indicators preserved absent history. Exact recorded-label groups
were disjoint across partitions. Development fitting used 2,314 eligible examples
from 2021–2023 and model selection used 83 held-out labels from 2024, before scoring
the later evaluation windows. Neither depth-2 nor depth-3 XGBoost met the frozen
validation improvement/A-recall rule. Persistence remained selected. [E21]

| Validation model | Macro-F1 | A recall |
|---|---:|---:|
| Training majority | 0.2310 | 0.0000 |
| Prior-year ABC persistence | 0.5367 | 0.5455 |
| XGBoost depth 2 | 0.4321 | 0.3636 |
| XGBoost depth 3 | 0.4347 | 0.3636 |

On 140 held-out full-year 2025 labels, persistence attained macro-F1 0.4379,
balanced accuracy 0.4591 and A recall 0.4211; B recall was only 0.0526. In the
separate 210-label January–September 2026 extension, macro-F1 was 0.3664,
balanced accuracy 0.3869 and A recall 0.1600; B recall was 0.0222. No unselected
challenger was refitted or scored on those later windows. Among these samples,
97 and 190 labels respectively lacked prior-year observations. Their fallback
predictions had zero A/B recall, showing the limited discrimination supported
by the available history. These are not stock-turnover or clinical-priority
findings. [E21]

The result is executed supporting research, not an untouched/blind test or accepted
SKU cold-start solution: source rankings/outcomes were already inspected, exact
labels are not verified SKU families, and historical catalog/publication availability
is uncertified. Temporal and held-out-label separation address a bounded
interpretation of the proposal, while all-catalog future-SKU ABC remains distinct.
Seventeen protocol/runner tests and separate saved-model/metric checks passed.
No warehouse, source, operational model, dashboard or publication state changed.
This negative challenger finding does not close original SO2 or establish that
XGBoost could never perform better under a different defensible design. [E21]
