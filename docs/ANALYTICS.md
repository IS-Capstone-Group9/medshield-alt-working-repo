#### 1. Produce the historical performance report

1. Open **Overview**, select **All Time · Yearly** and **Chart granularity →
   Granularity · Yearly**, and confirm the displayed history is 2017–2025.
   Keep the company/All population for the first report. Use **Export overall
   CSV** to retain the yearly financial series.
2. Choose **Custom Date Range**, January 1, 2017 through December 31, 2025.
   Set **Chart granularity → Granularity · Monthly** explicitly. Export the
   overall series again and retain a chart capture with dates, units and
   coverage visible.
3. Open **Product Prioritization** with the same dates; use **Export selected
   rows** for the chosen category/cohort. State that cohort explicitly: a top
   cohort export is not the entire product population. Use mapped-area outputs
   only with their unallocated share and mapping status disclosed.
4. Write a short factual result: annual movement, strongest/weakest recorded
   months, leading source-label contributions, and missing/adjusted evidence.
   Unknown months do not enter as observed zeros; estimated annotations do not
   become recorded actuals. Gross profit uses its own eligibility; margin uses
   the matching margin-eligible revenue basis.

**Save:** yearly/monthly CSVs, one historical chart and a one-page interpretation.
**Check:** exact selected dates and source identity agree; compare the same
eligible recorded population in API, chart, table and CSV. Scope changes or
estimates must be identified before comparing totals. The current source-linked
report is evidence, not final business/publication acceptance.

**Save:** one investigation JSON and three findings with evidence references.
