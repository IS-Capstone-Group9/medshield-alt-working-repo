## 3 Protocol Summary for Form 7.2 and the Cover Letter

Verify the exact Form 7.2 field labels against the current institutional copy.

### 3.1 Brief study summary

> MedShield is a historical analytics-driven decision-support system for pharmaceutical sales analysis, forecast evaluation, product and territory prioritization, and reviewed planning scenarios. The study uses historical sales and external context data to produce traceable descriptive, predictive-evaluation, and scenario outputs. It does not provide live disease surveillance, official weather alerts, medical advice, patient-level predictions, or automated procurement. Planning scenarios require human review before operational use.

Status: `DRAFTED`

### 3.2 Research problem

> Pharmaceutical planning is complicated by seasonal demand, uneven historical data quality, product and territory variation, and incomplete operational inputs. Existing planning processes may not provide one traceable view of historical demand, forecast performance, data limitations, and scenario assumptions. The study addresses the need for an evidence-based decision-support system that makes those distinctions visible without presenting unvalidated predictions or scenarios as approved operational decisions.

Status: `DRAFTED`

### 3.3 General objective

> To develop and evaluate a traceable historical analytics-driven decision-support system that supports pharmaceutical demand analysis, forecast evaluation, product and territory prioritization, and human-reviewed planning scenarios for MedShield Pharmaceutical Corporation.

Status: `DRAFTED`; revise if the approved Title Proposal uses different objective wording.

### 3.4 Specific objectives

1. To prepare and document a reproducible analytical dataset from authorized MedShield sales records for 2017-2025 and approved historical external data.
2. To describe pharmaceutical sales, demand, revenue, product, customer, territorial, and seasonal patterns while preserving data-quality and provenance limitations.
3. To evaluate forecast approaches using time-appropriate holdouts and comparable metrics such as MAE, RMSE, WAPE or sMAPE, and bias.
4. To generate traceable product and area priorities that distinguish observed, derived, estimated, and unavailable evidence.
5. To present constrained planning scenarios whose assumptions and limitations are visible and whose use requires human review.
6. `[CONDITIONAL Path B]` To assess the system's usability, understandability, and perceived decision-support value using an REC-approved evaluation with eligible participants.

Status: `DRAFTED`; map each final objective to a method, output, and analysis.

### 3.5 Research questions

1. What historical demand, sales, product, customer, territorial, and seasonal patterns are present in the authorized data?
2. How do eligible forecast methods compare on the same historical evaluation periods using the approved error metrics?
3. Which products and geographic areas emerge as planning priorities under the documented evidence and scoring rules?
4. What planning scenarios result from user-entered operational assumptions, and which constraints or data limitations affect those scenarios?
5. `[CONDITIONAL Path B]` How do eligible users assess the usability, understandability, and decision-support value of MedShield?

Status: `DRAFTED`

### 3.6 Study design

> The study is an applied system-development and retrospective analytical study. CRISP-DM guides business understanding, data understanding, preparation, modeling, evaluation, and deployment. SEMMA structures sampling, exploration, modification, modeling, and assessment within the analytics work. The secondary-data component analyzes authorized historical records. If Path B is approved, a separate minimal-risk system-evaluation component will collect participant feedback using the attached instrument and consent process.

## 5 Analysis Plan and Dummy Outputs

### 5.1 Descriptive analysis

- Counts, totals, distributions, trends, and year-over-year comparisons
- Demand based on approved quantity measures
- Revenue and transaction gross margin/profit using approved definitions
- Product and territory summaries
- Pareto analysis and ABC candidates
- STL seasonality only where continuous history is eligible
- Missingness, warning, rejected-row, mapping, and provenance summaries

### 5.2 Predictive evaluation

- Time-ordered, frozen-origin, or rolling-origin evaluation as appropriate
- Comparison on identical observable target periods
- Metrics including MAE, RMSE, WAPE or sMAPE, and bias
- Explicit reporting of unavailable forecasts and incomplete periods
- No automatic champion designation without the approved evidence and publication gate

### 5.3 External-signal analysis

- Historical disease and weather signals joined only through approved time and geographic mappings
- Lagged observational associations described as associations, not causation
- Separate labeling of official PAGASA observations and NASA POWER proxy data
- No live-alert claim

### 5.4 Planning scenarios

- User-entered assumptions for demand, stock, protected stock, pack size, supplier price, supplier capacity, and budget where applicable
- Integer-pack and constraint calculations presented as scenarios
- Human review required before use
- No automated purchasing or claim of operational optimization without the required real inputs and approvals
