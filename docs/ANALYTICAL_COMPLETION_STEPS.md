## 5. Compare models for supported series

**Owner:** analyst/model engineer. **Depends on supported Step4 inputs.**

Define target/unit, scope, issue date, calendar leads and evaluation cases first.
Use development rolling origins and transparent baselines. The offline routing
policy proposes regular/intermittent/short-history candidate families; it does
not select a winner from a name or guarantee that a complex model is better.
Preprocessing and pattern assessment use origin-available training history only.
Freeze choices before untouched final evaluation. The inspected2025 company
audit is not a new untouched test; report it honestly as retrospective evidence.

Report MAE/RMSE in the target's unit, WAPE where its denominator is nonzero,
signed bias and case/lead coverage. Quantity errors are units/percent; revenue
errors are pesos/percent. Error percentages do not replace their denominator or
absolute errors. Compare all candidates on identical origin/target/lead keys.
Do not default to overforecasting/underforecasting without the client's cost
contract. Current company-PHP historical error is unchanged by this plan.

**Done when:** reproducible source/run/artifact identities, common-case scores,
baseline comparisons, failed-case disclosure, leakage checks and appropriate
final evaluation are available. Future ABC/slow-mover and urgency classifiers
