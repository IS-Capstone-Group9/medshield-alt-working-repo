MAE is the average absolute peso miss per scored forecast; WAPE is total
absolute error divided by total absolute actual sales, multiplied by 100.
They use the same pairs and absolute-error numerator. They provide peso and
percentage interpretations, not independent votes. Neither caps the error in
every month. A zero absolute-actual denominator leaves WAPE unavailable; no
scored pairs leaves both unavailable. Signed bias and over/underforecast
magnitudes remain diagnostics. A small net bias can conceal large opposing
errors. The provisional per-peso absolute-loss weights remain **1:1**, without
a deliberate upward or downward forecast shift or measured business-cost ratio.

These are revenue forecast misses, not measured lost sales, cash loss, stock
shortages, excess purchases or expiry. The current revenue inputs cannot
determine medicine purchase quantities. Independent numerical QA establishes
arithmetic and artifact integrity; it does not establish client usefulness.
