## Chapter 4: Results and Discussion

This chapter presents the concrete outputs generated by the MedShield Decision Support System (DSS) and analyzes the performance of each stage of the analytics pipeline.

### 4.1. Data Ingestion & Quality Gatekeeper Results
* **ETL Lineage and Cleaning**: Show how raw sales inputs are mapped to the 13 canonical columns. Present data reconciliation metrics (e.g., accepted rows, duplicate logs, and isolated unit price anomalies).
* **Chronological Horizon Configuration**: Document the timeline boundaries: training on 2021–2025 data, validating model performance on 2026 actuals, and generating forecasts for 2027.

### 4.2. Descriptive Analytics Findings `[North Star 2A – 7A]`
* **Business Taxonomy & Contract Allocations `[2A]`**: Show how rule-based mappings group entries into physical regions and institutional channels. Present the backward-allocation results where bulk contracts (indicated by `#`) are split into estimated child product rows.
* **Historical Seasonality `[3A]`**: Present the monthly seasonality index ($SI$) results (e.g., demonstrating the recurring seasonal spikes of antipyretics during the June–October rainy months).
* **Pareto Concentration `[4A]`**: Discuss the 80/20 revenue concentration rankings, showing the minor share of Class A SKUs that drive the bulk of sales revenue.
* **YoY Growth & Geographic Trends `[5A & 6A]`**: Present the growth percentages and ranked territory leaderboards showing sales and net margin performance.
* **Customer Concentration `[7A]`**: Discuss the sales distributions across the Government, Hospital, and Pharmacy channels.

### 4.3. Predictive Analytics & Forecast Evaluation `[North Star 2B – 7B]`
* **Prophet Demand Forecasts `[2B]`**: Show the monthly demand forecasts for key SKUs, displaying expected volumes alongside shaded upper and lower confidence intervals.
* **Forecast Accuracy Comparison `[3B]`**: Present evaluation tables comparing the Prophet baseline model to naive benchmarks using MAPE, RMSE, and MAE.
* **Disease & Weather Regressors `[4B & 5B]`**: Analyze the model gains when incorporating epidemiological indicators (DLI/DII) and Rainfall Severity Index (RSI) regressors. Show how the weather-seasonal fallback rule handles missing DOH uploads.
* **XGBoost Prioritizations `[6B & 7B]`**: Show the predicted ABC class designations and continuous Demand Urgency Scores computed by XGBoost for cold-start (newly introduced) products.

### 4.4. Prescriptive Optimization & Decision-Support Outputs `[North Star 2C – 8C]`
* **Inventory Control Parameters `[2C & 3C]`**: Present calculated Economic Order Quantities (EOQ) and Reorder Points (ROP) paired with Safety Stock buffers.
* **Emergency Alert Overrides `[4C & 5C]`**: Document alert activations (e.g., showing how Dengue alerts override normal forecasts or how PAGASA storm signals double Safety Stock levels).
* **Stock Allocation & Priority `[2C & 6C]`**: Present MCDA priority scores and the resulting LP optimization matrices, showing how stocks are distributed under constraints. Detail how the DSS flags active binding constraints.
* **Product-Region matching `[7C]`**: List collaborative filtering recommendations based on high cosine similarity scores.
* **Stop-Purchasing Flags `[8C]`**: Present low-velocity Class C SKUs flagged as dead stock to halt procurement.

### 4.5. Research Limitations
* **Coverage Constraints**: Mention that official PAGASA data ends in 2024 and DOH data ends in 2025.
* **Proxy Data**: Highlight that NASA POWER/Open-Meteo observations serve as weather proxies rather than live official warning networks.
* **Scenario-Based Demonstration**: Clarify that due to incomplete operational parameter records in the database, the prescriptive recommendations represent scenario-based simulations for decision-support rather than finalized operational execution.

## Chapter 5: Summary, Conclusion, and Recommendations

This chapter synthesizes the overall project, matches outcomes against research objectives, and defines guidelines for future implementation.

### 5.1. Summary of the Study
* **Context**: MedShield needed to move away from static spreadsheets and manual processes to reduce medicine expiries and warehouse losses.
* **Solution**: Developed a unified analytics platform consisting of a Next.js interface, Python microservices, and a Supabase warehouse. The pipeline integrates data cleaning, time-series forecasting, XGBoost classification, and linear programming optimizations.

### 5.2. Conclusions
* **Addressing the North Star Goal**: Synthesize how the combined descriptive, predictive, and prescriptive modules answer the primary question of inventory optimization.
* **Key Takeaway**: Integrating meteorological and epidemiological signals into a structured DSS allows MedShield to anticipate regional demand spikes, prevent costly stockouts, and flag inactive dead stock prior to procurement.

### 5.3. Recommendations & Future Work
* **Operational Integration**: Integrate the DSS database directly with MedShield's live ERP and supplier inventory portals.
* **Live Feeds**: Establish real-time API integrations with official PAGASA weather warning feeds and DOH disease registries.
* **Dynamic Lead Times**: Apply predictive machine learning models to forecast shipping and customs delays during severe weather incidents.
