# MedShield Descriptive Analytics: Technical Logic & Process

> [!NOTE]
> **Data Horizon**: This system operates on a **2017–2026 historical baseline**. All descriptive analytics, seasonality indices, Pareto rankings, and YoY comparisons are computed from this full 10-year digitized transaction dataset. Forecasts generated by the predictive layer start from **2027 onwards**.

This explainer documents the mathematical logic and processing steps for the Descriptive Analytics layer of the MedShield Decision Support System (DSS). The methodology is grounded in the **MedShield North Star Diagram** and the analytical framework from the Group 9 ISB capstone research paper (`PRIVATE_SUMMER_CAPSTONE_2 - GROUP9_ISB.pdf`).

---

### 1. Ingestion & Quality Gatekeeper (Data Validation & Isolation)

* **Goal**: Validate data integrity and filter out unreliable records before any mathematical calculations begin.
* **The Process**:
  1. The system reads all raw sales rows from uploaded yearly source files, beginning from **2017** — the earliest boundary of the company's digitized transaction records.
  2. It parses the delivery date field. Rows with blank or unparseable dates are discarded.
  3. It checks the quality status column. Records marked as `rejected` are discarded.
  4. It checks the sales acceptance status column. Only records marked as accepted clean sales are retained.
* **Sparsity & Anomaly Handling**:
  * **Daily Sparsity**: Calendar days with no sales are not written as zero-demand rows; the system advances to the next active transaction date.
  * **Gross Margin Anomalies**: Rows with negative gross margin or margin exceeding total trade price are flagged as margin anomalies and isolated from company-wide margin calculations to prevent distortion.

---

### 2. Business Taxonomy Mapping (Rule-Based Encoding) [North Star 2A: Product, Territory, and Customer Grouping]

* **Goal**: Standardize raw transaction fields into geographic territories, customer channels, and product categories.
* **The Process**:
  * **Location Grouping**: Each location is looked up in the area classification directory:
    * **Physical Territories**: Provincial regions (Batangas, Laguna, Quezon, Bicol, Metro Manila).
    * **Customer Channels**: Institutional groups (Government, Hospitals, Pharmacies).
    * **Internal Business Lines**: Non-sales cost centers (Administration, Losses).
  * **Product Grouping**: Each SKU is looked up in the master mapping file. If unmapped:
    * The system scans the product name for office supply terms (pen, paper, folder, tape).
    * It cross-checks against a medical word whitelist (bandage, syringe, penicillin).
    * If no medical terms match, the product is categorized as a Non-Medical Operational Item; otherwise it defaults to Medicine or Medical Supply.
    * **Service Contracts**: Product names containing a `#` (e.g., `PAGBILAO # 13,500,000`) are classified as bulk service contracts (`is_service_contract = true`) rather than discrete medicine line items.
* **Downstream Impact**: Segregating office overhead and internal losses prevents them from distorting downstream forecasting and prioritization models.

---

### 3. Bulk Service Contract Breakdown (Backward Approximation) [North Star 2A: Service Contracts]

* **Goal**: Convert bulk area-summary service contracts into estimated product-mix rows without inflating historical revenue totals.
* **The Process**:
  1. Parent contract rows are identified using the `is_service_contract` flag.
  2. Because these rows represent bulk packages of unspecified medicines, they cannot be modeled as individual SKUs.
  3. The system scans the previous three years of transactions for the same area and channel to build a historical product-mix profile.
  4. The bulk contract's total quantity and revenue are allocated into five estimated child product rows based on those historical weights.
  5. Child rows are flagged as `estimated_backward_allocation` so they contribute to product-mix analytics while remaining clearly marked as estimates.
* **Downstream Impact**: Enables product-level exploratory analysis to include revenue from bulk service contracts while keeping data limitations visible to planners.

---

### 4. The Seasonal Demand Cycle [North Star 3A: Seasonal Demand Cycles]

* **Goal**: Calculate historical monthly demand index multipliers to identify recurring seasonal fluctuations across the 10-year baseline.
* **Variables**:
  * `Q` = sales quantity of a transaction
  * `N` = total number of months in the training period (2017–2026, up to 120 months)
  * `B` = overall monthly average baseline sales volume
  * `M` = average sales volume for a specific calendar month (e.g., all Januaries in history)
  * `SI` = Seasonality Index of a specific month
  * `SS` = Seasonal Strength (overall seasonal volatility)
* **The Process & Formulas**:
  1. Daily transaction quantities are aggregated into monthly sums across 2017–2026.
  2. Baseline (`B`) calculation:

     `B = Sum of Q / N`

  3. Monthly Average (`M`) calculation:

     `M = Average of Q for a specific calendar month`

  4. Seasonality Index (`SI`) calculation:

     `SI = M / B`

     * *Note*: Monthly indexes serve as descriptive validation benchmarks. Facebook Prophet decomposes time-series seasonality internally via Fourier series during the predictive phase.
  5. Seasonal Strength (`SS`) calculation:

     `SS = (Max SI - Min SI) / Max SI`

---

### 5. Revenue Contribution (80/20 Analysis) [North Star 4A: Revenue Contribution]

* **Goal**: Analyze the distribution of sales across the full 2017–2026 period to identify revenue concentration patterns.
* **Variables**:
  * `R` = product revenue (total trade price)
  * `R_total` = sum of all product revenues in the subset
  * `R_running` = running sum of revenue from the top-ranked product down to product `i`
  * `CS` = Cumulative Share for product `i`
* **The Process & Formulas**:
  1. Products tagged as Medicine or Medical Supply are isolated.
  2. Total revenue (`R`) is calculated per product and sorted in descending order.
  3. Cumulative Share (`CS`) calculation:

     `CS = R_running / R_total`

  4. Products are grouped into Pareto concentration bands:
     * **Top Band (Class A)**: Products generating the first 80% of cumulative revenue (`CS ≤ 0.80`).
     * **Middle Band (Class B)**: Products generating the next 15% (`0.80 < CS ≤ 0.95`).
     * **Bottom Band (Class C)**: Products generating the remaining 5% (`CS > 0.95`).
* **Downstream Impact**: Descriptive cumulative share rankings serve as the ground-truth training reference for the XGBoost ABC Classifier in the predictive layer.

---

### 6. Year-over-Year (YoY) Growth Trends [North Star 5A: Year-over-Year Growth]

* **Goal**: Measure structural demand growth or decline while removing normal seasonal effects across the 2017–2026 window.
* **Variables**:
  * `S_current` = sales value of the current target month
  * `S_prior` = sales value of the same calendar month in the prior year
  * `YoY_Growth` = growth rate
* **The Process & Formula**:
  1. Transactions are grouped into monthly buckets.
  2. Each target month is matched to the exact same calendar month in the previous year (e.g., June 2025 vs. June 2024).
  3. YoY Growth calculation:

     `YoY_Growth = (S_current - S_prior) / S_prior`

* **Sparsity & Anomaly Handling**: If a period within the 2017–2026 range has sparse or incomplete records (e.g., partial year data near the 2026 cutoff), the system flags it as partial and greys out direct YoY comparisons with a disclaimer to prevent false decline signals.

---

### 7. Territory Performance [North Star 6A: Territory Revenue and Net Income]

* **Goal**: Rank physical delivery regions across CALABARZON, Bicol, and Metro Manila by revenue and gross margin contribution.
* **Variables**:
  * `Revenue` = total revenue of a territory
  * `Net_Income` = gross margin amount of the territory
  * `Margin_Rate` = profit margin rate
* **The Process & Formula**:
  1. Non-geographic administrative records and internal losses are excluded.
  2. Remaining records are grouped by physical territory (Laguna, Batangas, Quezon, etc.).
  3. Revenue and Net_Income are summed per territory and ranked descending by total Revenue.
  4. Margin Rate calculation:

     `Margin_Rate = Net_Income / Revenue`

* **Downstream Impact**: Supplies raw Revenue Rank and Demand Growth inputs used in the prescriptive MCDA prioritization matrix.

---

### 8. Customer Concentration [North Star 7A: High-Value Institutional Clients]

* **Goal**: Map and rank the distribution of sales volumes across buyer channels to identify the institutional clients driving 80% of revenue.
* **The Process**:
  1. Transactions are grouped by institutional buyer channel (Government, Hospital, Pharmacy).
  2. Quantities sold and total revenue are summed per group.
  3. Channels are ranked from highest to lowest sales volume.
* **Downstream Impact**: Feeds directly into the prescriptive collaborative filtering system for product-region recommendations.

## Final QA Cross-Analysis Against the North Star & Capstone Paper

Alignment verified against the **MedShield North Star Diagram** (`references/NStar.md`) and Group 9 capstone research (`references/PRIVATE_SUMMER_CAPSTONE_2 - GROUP9_ISB.pdf`):

### 1. Data Baseline Scope (Paper Section 3.1.2 & 3.2)
* **Rationale**: The analytics baseline begins in **2017** — the earliest boundary of digitized company records. Manual records prior to 2017 (2013–2016) are excluded to preserve ETL integrity.
* **Updated Horizon**: This system incorporates **2026 actuals** as the final historical year, extending the original 2017–2025 training window by one full year for improved model stability.

### 2. Chronological Modeling Horizons (Paper Section 3.4.2)
* **Rationale**: The 10-year historical block (2017–2026) is the training dataset for all descriptive baselines, seasonality indices, and Pareto rankings.
* **Validation Period**: 2026 actuals serve as the final in-sample validation year.
* **Forecast Horizon**: All forward projections begin from **2027 onwards**, maintaining a clean separation between observed history and model inference.

### 3. Descriptive 80/20 vs. ABC Classifier (Paper Section 3.4.1.3)
* **Rationale**: The descriptive 80/20 logic computes cumulative Pareto percentages on historical products. This categorization supplies baseline training tags to the XGBoost ABC Classifier in the predictive phase — resolving the role division between descriptive sorting and predictive classification.

### 4. STL Decomposition & Seasonality Index (Paper Section 3.4.1.2)
* **Rationale**: Monthly Seasonality Indexes provide the multipliers needed to visualize periodic demand fluctuations aligned with the paper's Figure 3.38. Daily-to-monthly aggregation handles any sparsity in the 2026 tail data.
