How these metrics are calculated

Net sales revenue is sales after discounts (₱). Gross profit is the workbook margin amount before operating expenses, reconciled against net sales less acquisition cost. It is not company net income.

Gross margin % = total gross profit ÷ total net sales × 100; transaction percentages are not averaged. Zero revenue means the rate is unavailable. Unusual margins remain visible for reconciliation.

Units sold are delivered quantities in source units, a measure of fulfilled demand. Different pack sizes are not interchangeable; unfulfilled market demand is not measured.

MedShield DSS User & Defense Reference Guide

MedShield Decision Support System (DSS) provides pharmaceutical supply planners with intelligent, explainable recommendations:

  • Sales Diagnostics: Analyzes historical transactional demand across 2017–2025.
  • Product Prioritization: Pareto ABC matrix classification for inventory capital allocation.
  • Area Prioritization: Regional segmentation and MCDA weighted vulnerability ranking.
  • Forecast Modeling: 12-Month Prophet time-series projection with ±12.4% confidence intervals and weather-disease cross-correlations.
  • Prescriptive Planning: Solves EOQ & ROP safety stock buffers adjusted for seasonal monsoons and Dengue surge risks.
Capstone Presentation Note: All models feature live tuning, confidence bands, and auditable parameter overrides.
