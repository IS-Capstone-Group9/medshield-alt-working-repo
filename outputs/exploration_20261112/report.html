Forecast accuracy review

MedShield original itemized sales, contracts excluded. Records start in January 2017, but 10 months before 2020 are missing, so the forecasts use the unbroken run from January 2020 to September 2026 (81 months). Scored on 2022 to 2026 targets. Exploratory: every month was inspected by earlier studies; methods were chosen at each forecast date using only errors known by then.

Where the error comes down

Error (WAPE) is the average miss as a share of actual sales. Lower is better.

What fits MedShield's data

  • Routine sales (60% of revenue): 4-method blend, about 37% error 1 month ahead. Average of the 3-month mean, 6-month mean, 6-month median and growth-adjusted mean. Use for routine replenishment.
  • Within the month: day-20 estimate, 29% error, bias −2%. The largest single gain. Needs sales entered within days of delivery.
  • Annual budgeting: growth-adjusted 6-month mean. Error is a little higher month to month, but bias is near zero (+0.4% at 1 month ahead), so yearly totals are not understated.
  • Total sales, 1 to 6 months ahead: 4-method blend or 3-month mean, 49 to 62% error. Small gains over the reference; large orders cap accuracy.
  • Large orders: plan from frequency, not forecasts. Their own error is 68 to 89%.
  • Not a fit: ETS, Theta, trend lines and automatic method picking add little or increase bias. Building the total from routine plus a large-order allowance is worse (55% vs 49%).

Limits

  • Exploratory, not confirmed: confirmation needs months after September 2026, which no study has seen.
  • No method reaches the 10 to 15% error target; erratic high-value product families carry 73% of sales.
  • Pesos, not medicine units. Not a purchase quantity or approval.
