# Questions for the client

Prepared 13 November 2026 from the model register, the descriptive extensions and the forecast review. Part A is for
the team (why each question matters). Part B is a message to send. Nothing asks for buyer names, prices or the order and
tender list, which the client has said is confidential.

## Part A: what each answer unlocks

Ranked by how much each answer improves the analysis per minute of the client's time.

| # | Question | Why we need it | Unlocks |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Can you send sales for October 2026 onward, ideally monthly? | No months after September 2026 exist, so no result has been tested on unseen data | The forward test and all six queued re-tests; the day-20 estimate on the dashboard (needs uploads within days) |
| 2 | For your top 10 products, what is one unit in the quantity column (box, vial, piece) and how many pieces per box? | Quantities swing widely while peso sales stay steady (ENHAMOX: 45 to 1,570 per month), so units can't be used | Unit forecasts; safety stock and reorder points in packs instead of pesos |
| 3 | For Government and Hospital orders, which province were they delivered to? Province only. | 82% of sales has no delivery location | Territory analysis for most of revenue; the disease vs medicine test |
| 4 | Can you fill in the generic name for your top products? (worksheet attached) | Medicines are recorded by brand only | Disease-to-medicine links; unit planning by generic |
| 5 | Can you check 50 product groupings? (sheet attached; about 15 minutes) | The family mapping is AI-reviewed, not confirmed by someone who knows the products | Expert-validated family ABC–XYZ |
| 6 | What do the labels GOVERNMENT, HOSPITAL, ADMIN, EQUIPMENT and SUPPLIES mean, and why does GOVERNMENT not appear in 2018? | Channel labels changed between years | Reliable channel trends over time |
| 7 | Do records exist for the 10 missing months before 2020? | 107 of 117 months are recorded | A longer unbroken history for forecasting |
| 8 | Is a product breakdown available for contract sales (₱118.3M)? | Contracts are excluded from product and area analysis | Complete company sales; fixes most forecast bias |
| 9 | Our records show gross margin on medicine falling from 73% (2019) to 52% (2025). Does that match your experience? Is cost data complete? | Could be a real pricing or cost change, or a data issue | Confidence in the margin trend; a business finding for the client |
| 10 | Orders of ₱500,000 or more grew from 0% of sales (2017) to 60% (2026). Was this a deliberate move toward institutional tenders? | Explains why monthly totals became irregular | Context for the paper and the forecast limits |
| 11 | When a large Government or Hospital order is coming, how much notice do you usually get (days or weeks)? No order details needed. | Large orders can't be forecast, only prepared for | Realistic readiness rules for stock and cash |
| 12 | For a few steady products, what are typical supplier lead times, ordering cost and holding cost? Rough ranges are fine. | EOQ and reorder points currently run on entered example inputs | Real planning scenarios (SO4) |
| 13 | In 2025, sales were delivered late in the month (36% by day 20, against 59–74% in other years). Did anything change in delivery or invoicing? | Affects the day-20 estimate | Confidence in mid-month estimates |
| 14 | Which decision would you use this for first (monthly ordering, budgeting, tender readiness), and what level of error would still be useful? | No error tolerance has been agreed | A business-defined accuracy target instead of the assumed 10–15% |

**If the client can only answer three, ask #1, #2 and #14.** #1 enables every confirmatory test; #2 turns planning into
packs; #14 tells us what "accurate enough" means to her.

## Part B: message to the client

Subject: A few questions to finish the MedShield analysis

Hello,

Thank you for your support with the MedShield project. We have finished analysing the sales records you provided
(January 2017 to September 2026). A few answers from you would make the results more reliable and more useful for your
planning. Short or partial answers are welcome; please skip anything you'd rather not share. We don't need customer names,
prices or order details.

**Most helpful (if you only have a few minutes):**
1. Could you send sales for October 2026 onward in the same format? Monthly would be ideal.
2. For your top 10 products, what does one unit in the quantity column mean (box, vial, piece), and how many pieces are
   in a box?
3. Which decision would you most like help with first: monthly ordering, yearly budgeting, or preparing for large
   orders? And roughly how far off could an estimate be and still be useful (for example, within 20% or 30%)?

**Helpful if you have more time:**
4. For Government and Hospital orders, which province were they delivered to? The province is enough.
5. The attached sheet lists 100 products by brand. Where you know it, please add the generic name.
6. The attached sheet shows 50 product groups. Please mark each as correct or not (about 15 minutes).
7. What do the labels GOVERNMENT, HOSPITAL, ADMIN, EQUIPMENT and SUPPLIES mean in your records?
8. Our records show margin on medicines falling from about 73% (2019) to 52% (2025). Does that match your experience?
9. When a large Government or Hospital order is coming, how much notice do you usually get?
10. For a few regular products, what are typical supplier lead times? Rough ranges are fine.

Thank you very much.

[Your name]
MedShield Capstone Team
