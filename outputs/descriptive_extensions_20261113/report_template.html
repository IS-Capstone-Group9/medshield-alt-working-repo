Sales structure review

Eight descriptive views not yet on the dashboard. Original itemized sales, contracts excluded, January 2017 to September 2026 ( recorded months, net ). 2026 covers January to September. Every figure is a direct count or ratio; nothing is estimated.

1. Orders are getting larger

  • The median delivery document rose from ₱3,150 in 2017 to ₱15,260 in 2026.
  • Documents of ₱500,000 or more went from 0% of sales in 2017 to 49% in 2025 and 60% in January–September 2026.
  • Fewer, bigger institutional orders explain why monthly totals became harder to forecast.

2. Who buys has shifted between years

  • Government and Hospital labels together carry most sales in every year since 2019.
  • The mix swings sharply: Government was 69% in 2023 and 19% in 2024, when Hospital rose to 47%.

Channel labels come from the source workbooks and their conventions changed between years (for example, no GOVERNMENT label in 2018). Compare years with care.

3. Product category mix and margin

  • Medicine is the largest category every year, at 64–85% of sales. Medical supplies peaked at 21.5% in 2021, during the pandemic years, and were 12% in 2025.
  • Recorded gross margin on medicine fell from 73.4% (2019) to 51.5% (2025).

Each cell: share of the year's sales · gross margin %. Margin uses only rows where cost and sales both pass quality checks, and is before operating expenses.

4. The product portfolio turns over every year

  • Each year, 23–55% of sales comes from product families that did not sell the year before.
  • Concentration varies: the top 10 families carried 22% of sales in 2023 and 42% in 2026.

HHI (Herfindahl–Hirschman index) = sum of squared family shares × 10,000; under 1,500 counts as unconcentrated. Families use the candidate AI-reviewed mapping.

5. Returns, discounts and delivery timing

  • Returns and negative adjustments are small: 2.2% of sales at most (2020), near zero since.
  • Discounts fell from 1.7% of gross sales (2018) to 0.2% (2026).
  • By day 20, a typical month has delivered 59–74% of its sales, except 2025 at 36%, when deliveries clustered late in the month.

Limits

  • Recorded itemized sales only: contracts (₱118.3M) are excluded and 10 months before 2020 have no records.
  • Descriptive only: these views show what happened, not why, and do not predict.
  • Delivery labels mix channels and provinces; they are not verified buyer identities.
