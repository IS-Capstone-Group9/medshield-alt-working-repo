The first selected pass missed the known `DRDate` alias in two headers; only those two profiles were repaired. The date parser is an inventory heuristic: month-first on ambiguous slash dates, unlike the canonical modern Sales parser's day-first treatment. Monthly allocations and ambiguous date minima here are not financial reconciliation. The raw 2017 file has three unparsed nonblank dates and 55 blank dates, plus dates outside its nominal year; the raw 2025 CSV has one unparsed nonblank date and one blank. Workbook native dates and CSV strings can differ. Numeric `month` component columns in the disease/weather files are counted as unparsed by the generic date-field profiler; their complete `date`/`period` columns parse successfully. None of these observations justify editing, dropping or shifting source rows. Full counts, blank/unparsed fields and identities remain inspectable in `inventory.json`.

3. Preserve historical publication/availability times and revisions or snapshots for each proposed predictor. A 2026 extract timestamp or an event month does not establish availability at a 2023–2025 origin.
4. Review SKU, pack/unit and territory mappings before forecasting units. Verify disease/weather joins and origin availability; future weather or disease paths require forecast/scenario labels. This inventory identified no specific verified new signal that warrants another model proposal.

This assessment leaves the established revenue target and existing evaluated models unchanged. Business error costs and any approval tolerance remain a source-owner/business decision; this inventory introduces no threshold.
